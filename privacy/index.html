Privacy Policy

This page explains how Pound the Rock Limited handles information through this website and our products.

About us

Pound the Rock Limited is a New Zealand company developing digital products, including Space Times Tables.

This website

This website is designed to provide information about Pound the Rock Limited and its products. The first version of the site does not include user accounts, advertising, payment processing, analytics or tracking tools.

If you email us, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond to you or deal with your enquiry.

Space Times Tables

Space Times Tables is currently in development and testing. Before its public App Store release, this policy will be updated to accurately describe what information the released app collects, stores or shares.

We will aim to collect only information that is needed for the app to work and to be clear with parents about how children's information is handled.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Information may only be shared where reasonably necessary to operate our services, meet legal obligations, protect our rights, or where you have authorised us to do so.

Keeping information

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected for, or where we are required to keep it by law.

Your privacy rights

If we hold personal information about you, you may contact us to ask what information we hold or to request a correction, subject to applicable New Zealand privacy law.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Pound the Rock Limited at brendon@poundtherock.co.nz.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as the website and our products change. The date at the top of this page will show when it was most recently updated.
