Why Pound the Rock?

Keep at the work.

The name comes from the stonecutter idea: progress can be invisible for a long time, then one more hit finally cracks the rock. The point is not the final hit — it is all the work that came before it. Build, test, improve, repeat.

Useful first

We want to solve real problems simply, rather than add features for the sake of it.

Built by testing

Space Times Tables is being shaped through hands-on use, feedback and plenty of iteration.

More to come?

Maybe. We're starting with one product, learning as we go, and seeing where it leads.
