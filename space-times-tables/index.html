Space Times Tables

Work smarter, not harder.

See which multiplication facts your child struggles with, focus practice where it matters and know what to work on next.

Why Space Times Tables?

Focused practice. Clear next steps.

Space Times Tables helps children spend less time repeating what they already know and more time on the facts that need work.

Work smarter, not harder

A quick assessment identifies tricky facts, then targeted practice focuses on those that need work.

Play three games

Number Pop, Blaster and Block Match give children different ways to practise multiplication recall.

Set goals. Choose rewards.

Parents set learning goals and choose real-world rewards tied to their child’s progress.

Get visual help

Visual blocks show multiplication when an answer isn’t sticking.

See progress

Clear reports show what has been mastered, what needs work and where to focus next.

Practise off-screen

Printables give children another way to practise away from a screen.

No ads or sign-in

No advertising, account or sign-in is required. No personal information is collected.

Free

Assess and practise as often as you like, with no ads or time limit. Includes targeted practice, visual help, basic progress reports, Number Pop and one child profile.

Premium

Premium adds Guided Path, Blaster, Block Match, parent-set goals and real-world rewards, all Printables, detailed progress reports and multiple child profiles.
