Space Times Tables

A father-and-son mission to make times tables practice better.

Space Times Tables began as a project between a New Zealand dad and his eight-year-old son. We tried existing times-tables apps, worked out what we liked and what we wished they did better — and decided to build our own.

Find the gaps. Practise smarter.

The app finds the multiplication facts a child struggles with and focuses practice where it will help most. Learning comes first, with enough fun to keep children coming back.

Targeted practice

Quick assessments identify weaker facts so practice can focus on what needs work.

Simple for kids

Fast answer entry, visual help and straightforward practice designed for younger learners.

Useful for parents

Goals, rewards and progress reporting help parents encourage practice without hovering over every question.
